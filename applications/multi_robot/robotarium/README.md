# Multi-robot: Robotarium arena (Sec. 4.2)

Single-plan success of GlobMP on the Robotarium arena (5 maps x N in {3,6,9,12}) and the baselines it is compared
with, plus GlobMP behind a diffusion planner's projector interface (App. `sec:steering`).

## Files

```
projector.py          GlobMP as DiRecT's projector (BaseProjector.project): steering calls, final certified solve,
                      robot-by-robot certification with wall, timing and detour repairs
batch.py              all candidate teams of one projector call as one batched problem (CPU or GPU)
instances.py          map names, obstacle scales; load_robotarium; disc covers of boxes        (shared, identical copy)
evaluate.py           exact continuous-time robot-robot check and obstacle checks              (shared, identical copy)
figures.py            the multi-robot figures from result files (`figures.py robotarium`)      (shared, identical copy)
run/
  direct_hook.py      the three-line wiring of projector.py into the benchmark's DiRecT fork
  run_cell.sh         one Robotarium cell: diffusion sampler + GlobMP projector, the paper's configuration
  score.py            select (first certified candidate per instance), bench (the comparison's predicate),
                      exact (offline re-check without the benchmark)
data/robotarium/      instances.npz (10 per cell), maps.json, geometry.json (boxes in metres)
data/results/         robotarium_cells.json (baselines), robotarium_gpa_sr_one.json, robotarium_gpa_sr_one_detail.json
robotarium_baselines/ the baselines (DiRecT, CD, Final Proj., PCD-SHD, PCD-LB, MMD-CBS) on this arena: the forks, data
                      preparation, training, runners, scorer, models, the N = 12 upload scripts (robotarium_baselines/README.md)
```

`instances.py`, `evaluate.py` and `figures.py` are shared by both experiments; each folder keeps an identical copy,
so either folder runs on its own.

## Environments

* GlobMP side: Python >= 3.10 with numpy, scipy, torch (CPU is enough), matplotlib, and the `globmp` package
  on the path: `export PYTHONPATH=<repo root>` (or `pip install -e <repo root>`).  The runs behind the paper
  used one thread per job on pinned cores.
* Benchmark side (only for the benchmark's predicate, the instance export and the Robotarium sampler): the
  co-authors' reproduction of the DiRecT/MMD multi-robot benchmark (in `robotarium_baselines/`) with its `direct` conda env.  Two quirks:
  `PYTHONPATH` must put `multi_robot/` and `multi_robot/deps/torch_robotics` first, and `PYTHONNOUSERSITE`
  must not be set.  `EnvEmpty2D` has no checkpoint; `EnvEmptyNoWait2D` is used.

The baselines' code is in `robotarium_baselines/` (`robotarium_baselines/README.md`).  Their records
(`data/results/robotarium_cells.json`, a copy of `robotarium_baselines/eval/robotarium_cells.json`) were written by its
Robotarium scorer (`robotarium_baselines/eval/original/`; `robotarium_baselines/eval/score_cells.py` is the scorer for
new runs and reproduces them). The MMD-CBS cells are from the 2026-10-15 re-run with the isotropic normalizer
(`robotarium_baselines/eval/mmd_cbs_rerun.md`).

The trained single-robot models of the paper's setting (v2) are in `robotarium_baselines/checkpoints/<EnvClass>-RobotPlanarDisk/`
(`args.yaml` and the final EMA weights `checkpoints/ema_model_current_state_dict.pth`), byte-identical to the models
GlobMP's runs loaded.  Conveyor's is the wide-channel model retrained for `RBT_WIDE=1`; the other four are shared with
the earlier v1 setting.  Point the baselines' `MODELS_ROOT` / `--models-root`, and the `data_trained_models/` of the
fork behind `DIRECT_MR`, at this folder.  The planners rebuild their normalizer from the training demonstrations at
start-up; those (about 4 GB) are not included and are regenerated by `robotarium_baselines/data_prep/generate_data.sh`.

GlobMP is scored with the same predicates: robot pairs at least 2r apart at the 64 waypoints and the
benchmark's `task.get_trajs_collision_and_free` at its default interpolation (k = 0).

## Robotarium (Sec. 4.2)

The Robotarium numbers come from GlobMP used as the projector of the comparison's own diffusion sampler
(DiRecT with the single-robot models trained for the arena, r = 0.13 m, isotropic normalizer), on the
comparison's 10 instances per cell.  At each of the 25 projection calls of an instance the sampler's batch of
64 candidate teams is the reference of a tracking cost; intermediate calls steer (short restoration), the final
call solves every candidate team, and the returned plan is the first candidate GlobMP certifies (its
piecewise-linear motion is exact-checked against walls, arena, speed and 2r).  If no candidate certifies with
seed 18, fresh diffusion batches are drawn (seeds 19, 20, ...) and the first certified plan is returned.

```bash
export DIRECT_MR=$PWD/robotarium_baselines/DiRecT          # the baselines' DiRecT fork, with run/direct_hook.py wired in
export DIRECT_PY=<conda>/envs/direct/bin/python OUT=$PWD/rbt_out
run/run_cell.sh crossroom 12 0                       # map N gpu: 10 instances x 64 candidates
SEED=19 EXP_TAG=_s19i5 NINITS=1 INST_NPZ=<single-instance npz> run/run_cell.sh crossroom 12 0   # a retry
python run/score.py select --results $DIRECT_MR/results --certs $OUT --out ret
(cd $DIRECT_MR && <Robotarium env vars of run_cell.sh> $DIRECT_PY <here>/run/score.py bench crossroom 12 ret/returned_crossroom_N12.npz)
python run/score.py exact --returned ret --sr-json data/results/robotarium_gpa_sr_one.json
python figures.py robotarium --cells data/results/robotarium_cells.json --gpa data/results/robotarium_gpa_sr_one.json --out robotarium_sr_one_compact.pdf
```

The cells were run on one A100 each with 40 CPU workers for the robot-by-robot certification; Conveyor N = 9
and N = 12 were split into five jobs of two instances (`score.py` SPLITS), and the retried instances are listed
in `score.py` RETRIES (Conveyor N = 12: instances 1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9; Cross-Room N = 12: instance 5).  Conveyor
N = 12 instances 3 and 4 never certified (seeds 18 to 24), which is the 0.8 in the figure.  Wall time per cell
ranged from minutes (Empty, small N) to several hours (Conveyor N = 12).  The comparison's predicate does not
require the goal to be reached exactly: the sampler pins only the start, and the returned plans end on average
4 to 5 cm (at most 22 cm) from their goals; the same holds for every method in the comparison.

### Hardware gallery

`robotarium_gallery.pdf` shows frames of the overhead videos of the real runs (12-robot ring instance,
Cross-Room and Conveyor) with tracked paths; it is drawn by the co-authors' `fig_robotarium_gallery.py` from the
videos and tracks, which are not part of this package.  The GlobMP plans executed there ("best picks") were
produced on 2026-09-24 by the projector above, on the antipodal ring instances of an earlier benchmark
version, with a selection loop over steering settings (keep-right, circulation `GPA_CIRC_MODE=reflect`,
`GPA_SHIFT_SMOOTH`, intermediate budget) that kept the first certified plan passing a review (channel use, path
length at most 1.3 times the straight lines, a clean replay in the Robotarium simulator).  The submission
scripts were generated by the co-authors' uploader.  `projector.py` supports those settings; the loop and the
uploader are not included.

## Validation of this port (2026-10-12)

Robotarium: `projector.py` + `batch.py` against the original projector, on identical inputs (Cross-Room N = 3
and N = 6, a steering call and a final call, including the robot-by-robot certification): outputs agree to
3e-15 in iteration-limited runs (each code differs from its own repeat by 1e-14) and to 6e-4 (normalized units)
in full runs, with identical certified teams.  The final-call certificate screen of `projector.py` on the saved
64 x 10 candidates of Empty N = 3, Cross-Room N = 6 and Drop-Region N = 12 reproduces the original certificate
logs on 30 of 30 instances (625, 267 and 314 certified candidates; same first certified candidate).
`score.py exact` on the 20 saved returned-plan files gives the published single-plan success on all 20 cells
(Conveyor N = 12: 0.8, instances 3 and 4).  An end-to-end rerun of the diffusion sampler was not done here (it
needs the benchmark's Robotarium fork and a GPU).

Figures from the files in `data/results/`: `figures.py robotarium` matches the paper's PDF.
